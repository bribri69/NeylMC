# **⛏️ NeylMC Project**

_Elevate your Minecraft adventure with NeylMC, a carefully curated modpack designed to enhance performance, immersion, and quality of life.\
Whether you're exploring, building, or surviving, NeylMC provides the tools you need for a smoother and more dynamic gameplay._

**✨ Key Features :**\
**Performance Boost :** Optimized mods to improve FPS, reduce lag, and ensure a buttery-smooth experience.\
**Dynamic Lighting :** Experience realistic lighting with dynamic shadows that react to your surroundings.\
**Minimap :** Never lose your way with an intuitive in-game minimap for easy navigation.\
**Proximity Chat :** Engage in immersive roleplay or multiplayer interactions with a proximity-based chat system.\
**Fresh Animations :** Enjoy fluid and modern animations that breathe new life into Minecraft’s classic movements.\
**Quality of Life :** A suite of QoL mods including :
Just Enough Items (JEI): Effortless item and recipe browsing.\
AppleSkin: Track hunger and saturation at a glance.\
Better F3: A cleaner, more informative debug screen.\
Shulker Tool Box: Organize your inventory with portable, compact storage.\

**🎯 Why Choose NeylMC ?**\
NeylMC is perfect for players who want a balanced, optimized, and immersive Minecraft experience without overwhelming complexity. It’s ideal for solo adventures, multiplayer servers, or creative builds—all while keeping performance at its peak.
